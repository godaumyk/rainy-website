About Rainy

Rainy is a Scottish Straight cat, born on May 6, 2026. Unlike Scottish Folds, her ears stand up straight, but she has the same round, gentle face and easygoing personality. She loves curling up wherever the sun hits, follows her owner from room to room, and comes running the second she hears a treat bag crinkle.
